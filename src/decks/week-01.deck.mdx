# What Makes You Hate Something?

Designing Things People Hate

---

## A provocative title, a plainer question

If good design makes people's lives easier, what makes bad design so
effective at making them worse?

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> "A truly effective bad design does not necessarily look terrible."

---

## The instinct to resist

- ugly buttons
- garish colour
- confusing icons

All real. None of them are this course's subject.

---

## What this course is actually about

A bad design is bad because of what it makes you **do** —
not how it looks doing it.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Behaviour, not appearance

---

## The shape of the semester

Notice → Explain → Manipulate → Break → Measure → Design → Defend → Redesign

Eight verbs, twelve weeks, one system you'll come to know uncomfortably well
by the end.

---

## Today: the Hate Wall

Everyone names one thing they use and hate, and the exact moment it turns
from fine to frustrating.

---

## What happens to it

Every submission joins the semester's shared **Hate Catalogue** — the
dataset [Assessment 1](/assessments/hate-catalogue/) draws on.
