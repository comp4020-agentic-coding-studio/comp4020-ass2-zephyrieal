## Before the session

Bring your Hate Catalogue in the state you intend to submit it, plus the
neutral interface handed out in the previous session for today's exercise.

## In the session

The exercise is Make Me Click It: you're given a neutral interface and asked
to raise the probability that another student clicks one specific button. No
new functionality — only default selection, visual emphasis, framing,
ordering, urgency. Then the group rewrites their Hate Catalogue claims in the
same vocabulary: "the website made me choose the expensive option" becomes
"the website used a pre-selected default and visual emphasis to raise the odds
of the expensive option," which is a claim you can actually test later in the
semester.
