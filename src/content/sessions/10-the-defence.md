## Before the session

Nothing to prepare beyond an open mind — you'll be assigned someone else's
hated design in the room.

## In the session

The Defence: you're handed a design from the semester's Hate Catalogue that
you did not submit, and your job is to argue "this is actually good design" —
even, especially, if you still hate using it. Airport security, two-factor
authentication and speed bumps are the standing examples of
frustrating-but-defensible design; the goal is finding your own.

## Afterwards

Bad experience is not the same claim as bad design, and good experience is
not the same claim as good design. This is the distinction
[the Redemption Project](/assessments/redemption-project/) needs you to hold
onto for the rest of the semester.
