## Before the session

Nothing to prepare — the point of The Maze is arriving without a strategy.

## In the session

The objective is one sentence: change your address. The process underneath
it is not — deliberately conflicting instructions, unnecessary forms, unclear
terminology, information you already gave it once. You attempt the task, then
the class analyses exactly where it created failure, and why no amount of
visual polish on any one screen could have fixed it.

## Afterwards

A beautiful interface cannot save a terrible process — keep that claim;
[week 9](/sessions/09-human-guinea-pigs/) is about proving whether your own
bad designs actually work, on someone other than you.
