## Before the session

Bring the finished Redemption Project and a category you'd nominate it for —
Most Confusing, Most Manipulative, Most Unnecessary, Most Beautifully
Terrible, Most Effective, Most Defensible, Worst Physical Object, Worst
Interface, Worst Process, or one of your own.

## In the session

The last session runs like an awards ceremony rather than a lecture: every
student presents their Redemption Project, the class nominates and awards the
categories above, and the semester closes on the same question it opened
with — what makes something genuinely bad? There is deliberately no single
answer.

## Afterwards

[The Redemption Project](/assessments/redemption-project/) is due today.
That's the course.
