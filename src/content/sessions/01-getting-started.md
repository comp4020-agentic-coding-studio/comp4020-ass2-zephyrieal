## Before the session

Bring three examples: ordinary things you use regularly and hate — a ticket
machine, a subscription, a printer, a self-checkout, a door that lies about
which way it opens. Ugly doesn't count on its own; you need the moment each
one goes wrong for you.

## In the session

A short orientation first — the toolchain, the site, how the semester is
graded — then straight into the Hate Wall: each student names their three
examples and the exact point where each turns from fine to frustrating. The
class sorts submissions by what the user experiences, not yet by why it
happens; that question waits for [week 2](/sessions/02-first-review/).

## Afterwards

Every submission joins the semester's shared Hate Catalogue, which becomes
the dataset [Assessment 1](/assessments/hate-catalogue/) draws on. Keep your
example in mind — you'll come back to it before that's due in week 3.
