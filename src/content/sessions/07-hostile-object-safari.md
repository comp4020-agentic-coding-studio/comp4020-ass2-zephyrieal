## Before the session

Go looking, ideally on campus or on your way in: doors, chairs, packaging,
transport, bathrooms, signs, vending machines, road layouts, furniture and
kitchen appliances all count.

## In the session

Hostile Object Safari: for each of five objects, answer what it looks like
you should do, what you actually have to do, and why the difference is
confusing rather than merely awkward. Affordances and expectations exist
everywhere design does — this is the week the course stops pretending
"interface" means "screen."

## Afterwards

Hold onto your five objects; [week 8](/sessions/08-the-maze/) treats an
entire process the same way you just treated a door.
