## Before the session

Have your Worst Interface ready to hand to someone else, and a plan for what
you'll measure: completion time, errors, abandonment, confusion, success
rate, clicks, perceived difficulty.

## In the session

Human Guinea Pigs: you test your own bad design on a classmate who hasn't
been told what's being measured, and you watch in silence. Being annoying and
being effective are not the same claim — the entire point of this exercise is
finding out whether your interface produces the behaviour you predicted, or
whether people are more resourceful, more stubborn, or more oblivious than
your design accounted for.

## Afterwards

[The Hostile Design Experiment](/assessments/hostile-design-experiment/) is
due today. Designing something badly in theory turns out not to guarantee the
experience you imagined — which is exactly what week 10 asks you to reckon
with.
