Idris runs every session from week 2 onward — the workshop half of the course,
where the Hate Catalogue turns into an actual bad interface and then into
evidence. Background is in human factors research, mostly usability testing
for clients who did not enjoy hearing the results; that's the instinct they
bring to Human Guinea Pigs in week 9, where "does it actually work" replaces
"does it feel annoying" as the standard.

Ask during sessions first — most workshop questions are faster resolved live,
in front of the thing you're building. Email is for anything time-sensitive
between sessions: an extension, a materials question, or a Worst Interface
idea you want a sanity check on before you commit a week to it.
