## The brief

> Choose a real system you hate, work out why it's actually built that way,
> test it, then build and defend something better.

This is not "here is my nicer UI." It's an argument in five parts: why the
original system is bad (autopsy), why someone might have built it that way
anyway (defence), what happens when you actually test it (experiment), what
you changed (redesign), and why the new version is better (defence of the
redesign). The scope should fit twelve weeks of work, not twelve months — a
small system done thoroughly beats an ambitious one done halfway.

## What you submit

Five sections, in the order above, covering one system throughout: Autopsy,
Defence, Experiment, Redesign, and a final Defence of the redesign. Include
whatever evidence a marker needs to follow your experiment's results — raw
notes are fine; a polished report is not required beyond what makes the
argument legible.
