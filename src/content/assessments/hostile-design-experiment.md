## The brief

> Test your Worst Interface on a real person, and find out whether it
> actually works.

Being annoying and being effective are different claims. Run a small test —
one or a few participants is enough — measuring whatever is appropriate to
your interface: completion time, errors, abandonment, confusion, success
rate, number of clicks, perceived difficulty. Observe without intervening,
and record what the participant did, not just what they said afterwards.

## What you submit

Your test plan, what you measured and how, your results, and an honest
account of where they matched your prediction and where they didn't. The
most useful sections of this report are usually the ones describing what a
participant did that you did not anticipate.
