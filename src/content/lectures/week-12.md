The course opened by asking what makes something genuinely bad, and it
closes by asking the same question with twelve weeks of evidence behind it —
observation, psychology, manipulation, creation, testing, ethics, redesign.
There is deliberately no single answer by the end; there's a considerably
better set of tools for arguing about one.
