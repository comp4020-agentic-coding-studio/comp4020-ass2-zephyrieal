The worst interface might not be an interface at all — it might be a
process. University administration, government forms, insurance claims and
refund requests are all systems where the rules, not any single screen, are
what a person has to navigate. This week's claim is a hard one to argue with
once you've lived it: a beautiful interface cannot save a terrible process.
Polishing the form does nothing if the sequence of forms behind it is the
actual obstacle.
