Not all difficulty is equal. A password requirement can protect an account —
useful friction — or it can demand a reset every week for no defensible
reason — destructive friction wearing the same clothes. This week gives you
the distinctions that separate them: useful, accidental, deliberate and
destructive friction, and the idea that friction is something you can map,
place and measure rather than just suffer.
