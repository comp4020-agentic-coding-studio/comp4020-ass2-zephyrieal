Weeks 1 through 4 were analytical: recognise, explain, name. Week 5 is the
first exercise that asks you to build, and the constraint that makes it hard
is the one worth sitting with — make it significantly worse, without
removing any functionality and without actually breaking it. Deleting a
feature is not a design decision in the sense this course cares about;
introducing friction that a user has to work through is.

## Outline

- the constraint that makes this hard: worse, not broken, not missing
- the menu of frictions: cognitive load, hidden information, ambiguity,
  unnecessary decisions, waiting, punishing errors
- [this week's session](/sessions/05-make-it-worse/), and what carries into
  week 6
- what [Assessment 2](/assessments/worst-interface/) asks you to hand in
