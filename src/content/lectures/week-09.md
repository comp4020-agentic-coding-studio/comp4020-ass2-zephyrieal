Being annoying and being effective are not the same claim, and this is the
week that finally forces the distinction. Everything you've built so far was
designed to be frustrating in theory; now you find out whether it actually
is, on someone who wasn't told what was being tested. People are, on the
whole, more resourceful and more stubborn than a design brief tends to
assume, and this week's evidence is usually the most humbling of the
semester.

## Outline

- what to measure: completion time, errors, abandonment, confusion, success
  rate, clicks, perceived difficulty
- why a silent observer is the point, not an oversight
- a failed prediction is still a result — it's what tells you the
  interface didn't do what you thought
