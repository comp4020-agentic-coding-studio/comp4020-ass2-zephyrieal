Redesigning something is easy if you're allowed to ignore why it existed in
the first place. This week is harder than that: fix the system without
destroying whatever legitimate goal it was actually serving. That means
naming what the user needs, what the organisation needs, what constraints
were real, and what specifically caused the original problem — before you're
allowed to claim your version is better.
